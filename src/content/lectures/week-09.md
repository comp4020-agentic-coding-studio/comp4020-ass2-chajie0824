## Outline

- The new "employee-like worker" category: a deliberate middle category,
  neither employee nor ordinary contractor, built specifically for platform
  work
- What the Fair Work Commission can now set — minimum standards orders on
  pay and conditions for that category — and what it still can't touch
- Early sector response: which platforms adjusted terms proactively and
  which waited for a determination, and what that difference tells you about
  each platform's legal exposure calculation
