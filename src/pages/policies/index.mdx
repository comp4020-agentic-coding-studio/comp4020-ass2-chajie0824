# Policies and support

## Late work

Work submitted after the due time loses 5% of the available mark per day,
to a maximum of five days late. After five days a submission is marked as
zero unless an extension was granted in advance. Field notes are the one
exception: a note more than 48 hours late no longer describes "this week,"
so it is not accepted late at all — submit what you have on time.

## Extensions

Ask before the due time, not after. A short, specific reason is enough — you
do not need to disclose more than you're comfortable with. Extensions are
granted for the assessment as a whole, not per criterion.

## Academic integrity

Your fieldwork must be your own observations of a real app on a real order —
do not fabricate an ETA reading or invent a workaround you didn't document.
Quoting or summarising the 困在系统里 case, the Fair Work determinations, or
any other source without attribution is treated as a breach, whether or not
it was intentional. When in doubt about how to cite something, ask rather
than guess.

## Getting help

Questions about a week's content or an assessment brief go to the teaching
team listed on the [people](/people/) page. If a question affects more than
one person, ask it publicly rather than by email — the answer usually
belongs to everyone in the room.
