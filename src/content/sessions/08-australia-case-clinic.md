## Before the session

Read the assigned pre-reform Fair Work Commission case, Gupta v Uber Eats
[2020] FWC. Name the jurisdictional test the Commission actually applied —
not the multi-factor employment test in general, the specific reasoning this
determination used — and identify one fact that, had it been different,
would plausibly have changed which way the case went.

## In the session

Working through Gupta v Uber Eats in detail as a clinic: what was argued,
what control-versus-independence test the Commission applied, what the
ruling did and didn't settle about platform work, and what a different fact
pattern (a fixed shift, an exclusivity clause, a company-owned vehicle)
would have needed to succeed.

## Afterwards

Keep your notes on this case's limits — week 9 asks what the 2024 Closing
Loopholes reform changed about exactly this gap.
