Sam came to this course sideways, via two years reading submissions for a
gig-economy minimum-standards inquiry. Most of that job was noticing how many
different things "the algorithm made me do it" was being used to mean, and how
rarely anyone on either side of the argument had actually read the dispatch
code they were arguing about.

Sam runs the second half of semester — the weeks where the China case study
turns into a comparative question about Australia and the EU — and supervises
the capstone studio, where you have to propose a dispatch system rather than
just criticise one.

Consultation hours are posted weekly. Bring a specific question, not a request
to "look over" a draft with no direction — sessions run faster and better that
way for everyone in the room.
