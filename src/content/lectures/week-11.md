## Outline

- Design justice's core commitment: whoever bears a system's risk should
  have had a say in designing it, and the practical methods (worker
  interviews, co-design sessions, contestable defaults) that commitment
  implies for a dispatch system specifically
- What this would change about the mechanisms studied all semester — an ETA
  model, a lateness penalty, a rating threshold — if riders had been in the
  room when each was specified
- The capstone brief: propose one mechanism, name its tradeoffs, and defend
  it against the strongest objection a regulator from any of the three
  regimes could raise

## Before the session

Choose the single mechanism you'll redesign for the capstone and bring a
one-sentence statement of who currently bears the cost of how it works now.
