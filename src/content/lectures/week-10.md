## Outline

- The EU Platform Work Directive's central mechanism: a rebuttable
  presumption of employment, which flips the burden of proof onto the
  platform rather than requiring the worker to establish employee status
- Building a typology across the three regimes along one axis — who has to
  prove what, to whom, before any protection applies — rather than ranking
  them by how protective they sound
- Why each regime's design choice tracks its own legal tradition and
  political economy: a presumption-based model fits a civil-law system's
  instincts differently than a case-by-case common-law one
