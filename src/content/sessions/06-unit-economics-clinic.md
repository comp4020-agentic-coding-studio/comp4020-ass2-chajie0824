## Before the session

Bring your field notes in near-final form.

## In the session

Half the session builds an order's unit economics from published fee
schedules and commission rates; the other half runs peer reads of field
notes in pairs, checking each note against the "specific claim, specific
example" standard from the brief.

## Afterwards

Field notes are due Friday at noon — this is the last structured feedback
before submission.
