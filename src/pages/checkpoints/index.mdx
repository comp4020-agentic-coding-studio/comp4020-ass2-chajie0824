Ten checkpoints, weeks 2–11, worth 2% each: 20% of the mark in total,
alongside the 80% carried by the three assessments. Each one is small on
purpose — the point is that you did the specific piece of fieldwork the
week asked for, on time, not that you wrote well about it. See
[assessment](/assessments/) for the other 80%.
