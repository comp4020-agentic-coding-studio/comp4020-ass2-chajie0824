## Outline

- Individual workarounds: fake-GPS apps, deliberately refusing low-value
  orders to protect an acceptance-rate score, and the arms race each tactic
  starts with the next platform patch
- Collective tactics: regional WeChat and Telegram groups that share which
  restaurants are running long kitchen delays, effectively crowd-sourcing the
  information the platform's ETA model doesn't have
- Why individual and collective resistance produce very different platform
  responses — one gets patched quietly, the other gets escalated, and that
  asymmetry is itself evidence about where the platform sees its risk

## Before the session

Find one documented example (reporting, a forum thread, a patch note) of a
platform responding to a rider workaround. Bring what it tells you about what
the platform was actually worried about.
