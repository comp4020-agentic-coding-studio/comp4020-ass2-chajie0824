Pick one order — real or reconstructed from a receipt — and build its full
cost breakdown: what the customer paid, what the platform kept as commission,
any discount or subsidy applied, and what the rider was paid. Cite a
published rate or fee schedule for each figure rather than estimating.

Then answer: if the subsidy line disappeared tomorrow, who would absorb that
gap — the customer, the merchant, or the rider? Bring your working, not just
the total.
