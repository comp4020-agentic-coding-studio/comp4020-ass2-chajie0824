## Outline

- The Meituan–Ele.me subsidy war: what "burning money" actually bought, and
  why the spending stopped when it did rather than when profitability arrived
- Unit economics of one order: delivery fee, restaurant commission, customer
  discount and rider pay laid out side by side, so it's visible which party
  absorbs a price war's cost when the subsidy runs out
- Why a platform in growth mode treats rider pay as the flexible variable —
  not out of malice, but because it's the only line item not fixed by a
  restaurant contract or a customer-facing price
