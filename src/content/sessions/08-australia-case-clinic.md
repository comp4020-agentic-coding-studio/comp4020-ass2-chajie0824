## Before the session

Complete the [week 8 checkpoint](/checkpoints/week-08/): the jurisdictional
test the Commission applied in Gupta v Uber Eats, and one fact that would
have plausibly changed the outcome.

## In the session

Working through Gupta v Uber Eats [2020] FWC in detail as a clinic: what was
argued, what control-versus-independence test the Commission applied, what
the ruling did and didn't settle about platform work, and what a different
fact pattern (a fixed shift, an exclusivity clause, a company-owned vehicle)
would have needed to succeed.

## Afterwards

Keep your notes on this case's limits — week 9 asks what the 2024 Closing
Loopholes reform changed about exactly this gap.
