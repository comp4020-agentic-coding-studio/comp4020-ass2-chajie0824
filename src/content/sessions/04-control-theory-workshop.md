## Before the session

Pull one sentence from Rosenblat and Stark's account of Uber's rating system
as a disciplinary device. Hold it against one specific platform mechanism —
not necessarily Uber's — and note in one sentence whether Lee et al.'s
taxonomy sorts it cleanly.

## In the session

Groups are given a list of real platform mechanisms — an ETA display, a
rating threshold, a streak bonus, Meituan's late-delivery scoring penalty —
and sort each against the taxonomy, then present the one that resists easy
classification.

## Afterwards

The mechanism that didn't fit cleanly is good material for the
system-up-close essay.
