weekly checkpoints — 2% each, checked live at the start of the [session](/sessions/)
it belongs to, weeks 2 through 11.

| Item | Week | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The system, up close | 7 | 20% |
| Same algorithm, different country | 11 | 20% |
| Redesign the dispatch | 12 | 40% |
| Weekly checkpoints (×10, one per week 2–11) | 2–11 | 2% each (20% total) |
| **Total** | | **100%** |
