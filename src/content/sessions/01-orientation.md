## Before the session

Place one order through a delivery app — Uber Eats, DoorDash, or Menulog if
you're in Australia; Meituan or Ele.me if you have access to a China
account; Deliveroo or Just Eat if you're comparing an EU market. Screenshot
the ETA and the price breakdown the app shows you before and after you
confirm. No prior reading required — this session is about the shape of the
semester, not content yet.

## In the session

A short walkthrough of the assessment sequence — three essays
(system-up-close, same-algorithm-different-country, redesign-the-dispatch)
worth 80% between them, and ten weekly checkpoints worth 2% each — and how
each piece feeds the next. Then small groups compare screenshots: which app
showed a moving ETA, which showed a flat delivery fee versus a
demand-based one, and what got hidden until checkout.

## Afterwards

You should be able to say, without checking, which weeks belong to the China
case study (weeks 2–6) and which belong to the comparative-regulation half
(weeks 7–12).
