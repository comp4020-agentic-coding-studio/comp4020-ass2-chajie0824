## Outline

- Lee et al.'s taxonomy of algorithmic management functions (directing,
  evaluating, disciplining) as a checklist for naming what a given mechanism
  is actually doing, instead of calling all of it "surveillance"
- Rosenblat and Stark's account of ratings systems as informal, distributed
  discipline — the customer rates, the platform enforces, and no single actor
  has to own the decision
- Gamification as the other half: streak bonuses and badge-style incentives
  that recruit a rider's own motivation instead of monitoring it — and why
  that's a harder mechanism to argue against in a regulatory hearing
