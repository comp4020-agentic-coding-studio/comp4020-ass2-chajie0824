## Outline

- The piece itself: how it built its case from riders' own accounts of
  shrinking delivery windows, and why "the system" rather than "the company"
  became the accusation that stuck
- What happened next: Meituan and Ele.me's public responses (an "extra five
  minutes" toggle, in one case) and the gap between a PR response and a
  mechanism change
- Reading this as a case study rather than an exposé: which claims in the
  piece are independently checkable against patch notes, rider forums or
  reporting, and which are testimony you'd need to corroborate before citing
  in your own essay
