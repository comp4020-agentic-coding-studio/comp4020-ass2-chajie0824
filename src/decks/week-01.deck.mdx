# The algorithm has a boss too

Week 1 · Trapped in the System

---

## An order, from the outside

You order food. The app shows:

- a price
- an ETA
- a rider, moving on a map

Every one of those numbers was decided by someone. Not fixed by physics.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# This course is about who decided, and what it cost someone else

---

## Two halves of semester

1. **Weeks 1–6** — the mechanism, in China: dispatch, ETA, ratings, pay,
   and how riders route around all three
2. **Weeks 7–12** — the same mechanism, argued over in courtrooms and
   parliaments: China, Australia, the EU

---

## Why "trapped in the system"

《人物》's 2020 feature gave this problem its name in Chinese public
discourse: 骑手，困在系统里 — the rider, trapped in the system.

We will take that claim seriously enough to test it, not just repeat it.

---

## The central tension

- the algorithm is not a boss you can name
- but every parameter in it was set by someone who is
- "the algorithm decided" is a sentence that hides an org chart

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> An algorithm is not a rule from nowhere. It is a decision, laundered
> through code, made by someone who will never meet the person it falls on.

---

## What you'll build toward

- **Field notes** (wk 6) — six weeks of specific, checkable observation
- **System up close** (wk 7) — one mechanism, closely read
- **Same algorithm, different country** (wk 11) — China vs Australia vs EU
- **Redesign the dispatch** (wk 12) — propose a mechanism, defend it
