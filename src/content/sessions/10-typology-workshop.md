## Before the session

Draft the paragraph you intend to open your comparative essay with. Name one
axis — worker classification, minimum pay, or algorithmic transparency — and
state, in that paragraph, why China, Australia, and the EU land differently
on it, not merely that they do.

## In the session

Round-robin peer feedback in small groups, checking each draft against one
question: does this axis explain a difference — why the EU's Platform Work
Directive presumes employment where Gupta v Uber Eats didn't — or just list
one? Revise between rounds.

## Afterwards

Your revised axis should be usable as the opening paragraph of the essay
due next week.
