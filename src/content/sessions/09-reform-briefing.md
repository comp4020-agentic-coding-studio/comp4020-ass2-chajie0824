## Before the session

Take two provisions from the 2024 "employee-like worker" minimum standards
order and map each onto a specific mechanism the course has already covered
— the ETA model from week 2, the scoring penalty from week 4, a deactivation
trigger, or similar. Then name one mechanism from those weeks that the order
still doesn't reach. Bring the mapping as a short table: provision,
mechanism, covered or not.

## In the session

Small groups build a shared table mapping order provisions — the minimum
pay floor, the deactivation-notice requirement, the collective-bargaining
access clause — onto specific platform mechanisms from your week 6 unit
economics breakdown and week 2 ETA log, then identify gaps: what a rider
affected by one of those mechanisms still isn't covered for.

## Afterwards

This table is useful raw material for the comparative essay due in week 11.
